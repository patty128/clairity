# Clairity Glossary

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved terminology baseline

---

## Association

A repeated or measurable relationship between two recorded factors. An association does not establish that one factor caused the other.

## Autosave

Immediate persistence of each valid input without requiring a separate Save, Submit or Finish action.

## Behaviour

An action that occurred, such as smoking or binge eating. It is distinct from an urge.

## Calculated result

A value derived from other evidence according to a documented calculation, such as total sleep duration or a moving average.

## Completion marker

An optional organisational signal indicating that Claire considers a period sufficiently recorded. It does not control persistence or data validity.

## Context

Information that may help explain an event or outcome, including travel, illness, routine disruption, exposure, social events or unusual circumstances.

## Core evidence

The stable set of measures required for comparable analysis. Presentation may adapt, but core evidence must remain available and must not be silently reduced.

## Evidence

A recorded, imported or calculated item that can support later review or analysis.

## Evidence strength

A plain-language assessment of how much relevant, repeated and sufficiently complete evidence supports an insight.

## Hypothesis

A possible explanation consistent with available evidence but not established as fact.

## Insight

A generated summary that helps interpret evidence. It may be descriptive, associative or hypothetical and must state limitations.

## Measure

A defined evidence field within a module, including its type, scale, unit and missing-data semantics.

## Missing data

An expected or potentially useful evidence value that was not recorded. Missing does not mean zero, none, neutral or unchanged.

## Module

A self-contained evidence or life-stage area registered through shared platform services, such as sleep, nutrition or smoking.

## Observation

A descriptive statement directly supported by recorded or calculated evidence.

## Outcome

A result or state being examined, such as a symptom, appetite change, weight trend or disengagement.

## Recorded fact

Information Claire explicitly entered or that was imported from an identified source. It may still be estimated or corrected.

## Retired module

A module no longer active for current logging whose historical evidence remains preserved and reviewable.

## Retrospective entry

Evidence entered after the time it describes. The system preserves both the event time and recorded time.

## Source

The origin of evidence, such as manual entry, import or calculation.

## Urge

A desire or impulse to perform a behaviour, recorded separately from whether the behaviour occurred.
