## Rule

An item appearing here is **not a product requirement**. It may be researched, prototyped or promoted only after an explicit decision. Promotion requires:

1. feasibility assessment;
2. product approval;
3. Decision Log entry;
4. Product Requirements update;
5. architecture and design updates where affected.

## Status labels

- **Discovery:** Problem or opportunity needs investigation.
- **Proposed:** Reasonably defined but not approved.
- **Deferred:** Valuable, but deliberately postponed.
- **Blocked:** Depends on another decision or capability.
- **Rejected:** Retained only to preserve decision history.
